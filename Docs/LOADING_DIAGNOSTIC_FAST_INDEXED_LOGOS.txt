Experimental loading comparison / Comparaison experimentale du chargement

English
This variant retains the optimized VM filter from the four-group variant.
Enabled: ENHANCED, wheel/mouse switching, map controls, General Improvements,
Vehicle Enhancements and Skip Startup Logos.
Disabled: steering wheel force-feedback improvements.
Saved preferences are preserved; this diagnostic build forces the selection above.
Compare the same save against the previous optimized four-group variant
(about 33 seconds on the test machine). Measure from Continue until the save
is playable, separately from the skipped startup logos.
Verify the logos are skipped and the four groups still work.
The report is SnowRunnerEnhanced.loading-diagnostic-fast-features-1f-indexed.status.json.
Close the game before replacing the ASI. Keep the previous ASI for rollback.
Automated tests do not replace this in-game comparison.

jusqu'a la partie jouable, independamment des logos ignores au demarrage.
Verifier que les logos sont ignores et que les quatre groupes fonctionnent.
